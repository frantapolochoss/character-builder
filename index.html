Why your AI character keeps changing

You make a great image of your character. Then you ask for the same character in a kitchen, and suddenly the hair is a different colour, the glasses are gone and the face looks like someone else. This happens to almost everyone, and it is not your fault. It comes from how AI image models work.

The model does not remember your character

Every prompt is a fresh start. The model has no memory of the image you made five minutes ago. It only sees the words you give it right now. If those words are short or vague, like "a fat guy with glasses", the model fills in the missing details randomly – and random details are different every time.

Small wording changes create a new person

"Messy brown hair" and "untidy brunette hair" mean the same thing to you, but to the model they are different instructions. Change a few words and you get a slightly different character. Change many and you get a stranger. That is why a locked description works: you write the character once, in detail, and paste the exact same paragraph every time.

Scene words fight the character words

If your prompt says "a man at the gym", the model may add muscles and sportswear because that is what gym images usually show. The fix is to state clearly that the outfit and body stay the same, and to describe the scene separately from the character. The prompts this tool creates are built this way: character first, then scene, then a short "keep the same" reminder.

Style must be locked too

A character drawn in anime style and then in 3D cartoon style will never look like the same person. Pick one art style and keep it in every prompt.

Extra tips

  • Pick 3–5 strong details that are easy to recognise (round black glasses, a stained grey T-shirt). Strong details survive better than many small ones.
  • If your tool allows a reference image, upload your best image of the character together with the locked description.
  • Generate a few versions and keep the one closest to your character. Even good prompts need some picking.
  • Save your locked description in a note so you can reuse it for every episode.

FAQ

Is the Consistent Character Builder free?

Yes. It is completely free, needs no account and runs only in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Which AI image tools does it work with?

The prompts are plain text, so they work with Google Flow, Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram, ChatGPT image generation, Stable Diffusion and most other image or video generators.

Will my character look 100% identical every time?

No tool can promise that. A locked description makes your character much more consistent. For even better results, also use a reference image of your character if your tool supports it.

Why should I keep the description exactly the same?

AI models read every word. If you change the wording even slightly, the model treats it as a new person. Copying the exact same paragraph into every prompt gives it the same instructions every time.

Can I use this for a cartoon series?

Yes. That is what it was made for: one recurring character across many scenes and episodes.
